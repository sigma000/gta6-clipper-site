GTA6 Clipper

GTA6 Clipper is a personal tool used by the ninja yt channel to publish short, credited highlight clips of GTA 6 gameplay from streamers who allow clipping (for example through official clipping campaigns).

What it does

  • Finds popular GTA 6 clips from approved streamers on Twitch.
  • Formats them as vertical short videos with captions and a visible credit to the original streamer.
  • Uploads them to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X accounts through each platform's official API.

The tool only posts to accounts owned by its operator. It does not collect data from other users.
